Frequently asked questions

Are these prices live?

No. This starter site uses example prices and sample product data. Replace them with verified details and clearly show when prices were checked. Do not claim live price tracking unless you have built and tested it.

How can this website earn money?

Possible options include approved display ads, affiliate links to retailers, and sponsored placements clearly labelled as sponsored. Approval, traffic, disclosure and local legal requirements apply. Earnings are not guaranteed.

Can visitors buy products here?

Not in this starter version. It is a comparison demo. Add genuine retailer links or an approved affiliate feed before inviting visitors to shop.

What privacy information should I publish?

Publish a real privacy policy describing the data you collect, cookies, analytics, advertising partners, contact details and user rights. Update it to match the services you actually use and the laws that apply.
